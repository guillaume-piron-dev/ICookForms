## DIFFÉRENCES CCPA vs RGPD

| Aspect | CCPA/CPRA | RGPD |
|--------|-----------|------|
| **Consentement** | Opt-out (sauf sensitive data) | Opt-in requis |
| **Scope** | Résidents Californie | Résidents UE |
| **Droits** | Know, Access, Delete, Opt-out | Access, Rectify, Erase, Port, Object |
| **Sale** | Right to opt-out of sale | Consent required for sale |
| **GPC** | Must honor GPC signal | No equivalent |
| **Penalties** | $2,500-$7,500 per violation | 4% global revenue or €20M |
